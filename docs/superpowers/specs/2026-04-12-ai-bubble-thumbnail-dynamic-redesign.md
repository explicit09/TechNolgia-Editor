# AI Bubble Thumbnail Dynamic Redesign

## Goal

Redesign the existing `Thumbnail — The AI Bubble` Paper artboard into a more dramatic, asymmetric composition while preserving the established two-host format, title-led hierarchy, and green/black brand system.

## Why This Pass Exists

The current board is visually strong but too rigid. The centered slab and balanced layout create a template-like split-screen effect instead of a more premium, dynamic media thumbnail.

This redesign focuses on increasing motion, tension, and depth without abandoning the existing recognizable system.

## Scope

In scope:

- Rework composition to feel more asymmetric and energetic
- Reduce the dominance of the center slab
- Increase depth between subjects, slab, and text
- Keep the existing hosts, title, and branding system

Out of scope:

- Changing the episode title
- Changing the host names
- Replacing the host-photo source assets
- Rebranding away from the current green/black identity

## Approved Direction

Use a more dramatic redesign rather than a conservative polish pass.

This means:

- Prioritize energy over symmetry
- Introduce controlled imbalance and overlap
- Keep the visual system recognizable while making the composition feel less templated

## Composition

### Center Slab

- Narrow the center slab by roughly 10 to 15 percent compared with the current version
- Increase the taper so the slab feels more intentional and less like a vertical block
- Offset it slightly rather than leaving it perfectly centered
- Let it blend into the photo panels more softly so it feels integrated into the scene

### Photo Balance

- Keep the left host side calmer and visually heavier
- Move Elvis slightly inward so the right portrait feels more involved with the center composition
- Allow one side to feel marginally more dominant than the other to create energy

### Title Placement

- Keep `THE AI BUBBLE` as the hero element
- Let the title banner cut more assertively through the composition
- Allow the title system to overlap one side slightly more than the other instead of sitting in a perfectly centered safe zone

## Hierarchy

### Primary

- Main title
- Host faces

### Secondary

- Logo
- `WITH`
- `TADIWA & ELVIS`

The lower host-credit block should stay clearly secondary in this pass. It should be more refined, but not compete with the title.

## Depth Treatment

- Darken photo edges subtly to increase focus toward the center
- Keep both host faces crisp and readable
- Use the slab more as a tonal separator than a hard visual wall
- Keep green glow restrained and atmospheric rather than loud

The intent is to create stronger foreground, midground, and background separation so the thumbnail feels less flat.

## Geometry

- Reduce the perfect-rectangle feel
- Use stronger taper and overlap instead of relying on centered symmetry
- Keep the composition structured, but no longer safe-looking

## Credit Block

- Keep the `WITH` row
- Keep the green nameplate
- Make the green plate slightly smaller and tighter than the current version
- Improve spacing so the lower block feels deliberate and premium, but still secondary

## Constraints

- Do not discard the overall brand language
- Do not add decorative elements that dilute the existing strong identity
- Do not overcomplicate the layout with unnecessary motion devices
- Keep the result editable in Paper

## Validation

The redesign is successful when:

- The thumbnail feels more dynamic than the current centered version
- The slab supports the composition instead of dominating it
- The title remains the clear hero
- The host faces feel more integrated with the graphic system
- The overall image reads like a premium recurring media format rather than a rigid template
